# Why libSQL?

### SQLite: A Beloved Foundation  
SQLite is the **“Hello World” of databases**—simple, reliable, and zero-configuration. Developers love it, and its presence is nearly universal, embedded in countless devices worldwide.
While SQLite is open source, there's a catch: it doesn't accept community contributions. Think of it as a brilliant cookbook that everyone can read but nobody can add recipes to. Community-driven improvements often get stuck in silos.
This limitation has left many developers wondering, "What if?"

## Enter libSQL: The Next Chapter 📖

libSQL emerges as an MIT-licensed fork of SQLite, but with a crucial difference: it's built for collaboration. Here's what makes it exciting:

### The Vision
- **Full Compatibility**: Everything that works with SQLite keeps working
- **Modern Architecture**: Rust integration for new features
- **Community-Driven**: Open to contributions
- **Forward-Thinking**: Ready for distributed systems, async operations, and WASM
- **Linux-Kernel**: Could SQLite be embedded in the linux Kernel?
